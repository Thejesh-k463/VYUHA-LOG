# Narration — paste one block at a time into Clipchamp Text to Speech

Owner-only · written 2026-08-23 against v2.99.100 · every screen name below is
the exact label in `components/layout/nav-config.ts`, every claim is verified
against the code, and nothing here makes an outcome claim (SEBI posture).

**How to use:** Clipchamp → Record & create → Text to speech → English (India)
→ paste ONE block → speed 0.9× → generate → rename the clip `VO-n`. Eight
clips. Do not paste the whole file as one clip; alignment in the edit is far
easier with eight short ones.

**Reading notes for the voice:** full stops are pauses — leave them in. Em
dashes read as a beat. Numbers are written as words where the voice should say
them as words.

---

## VO-1 · The gap
*~14 s · pairs with SHOT 1*

Your broker told you your P and L. What they didn't tell you is what it actually cost you to get there — and it is rarely the number on the contract note.

## VO-2 · What this is
*~10 s · pairs with SHOT 2*

Vyuha is a trade journal for Indian retail traders that runs entirely on your own computer. No account. No cloud. Nothing leaves your machine.

---

## VO-3 · Import
*~22 s · pairs with SHOT 3 — the differentiator; this gets the most screen time*

Drop your broker file in. Six brokers are recognised automatically — by what is inside the file, not by its name. And here is the part that matters. Before anything is saved, Vyuha recomputes every charge from your own rate card, and shows its number right next to the broker's.

## VO-4 · One trade
*~13 s · pairs with SHOT 4*

Every fill becomes the trade you actually made. Same-day buys and sells are netted first; the rest are paired oldest first. Scaled in over a week? The ladder is kept, not blended into one average.

## VO-5 · The questions
*~24 s · pairs with SHOT 5 — one sentence per screen, four screens*

Then it starts answering questions. When am I actually good — and when am I just busy? Which setup earns, by expectancy, not by feel? Which broken rule costs me the most? And what am I exposed to, right now?

## What was deliberately left out, and why

| Cut | Reason |
|---|---|
| "improve your trading", "make money", any win-rate | Outcome claims. SEBI posture; also the whole pitch is that you *don't* do this. |
| "ten thousand trades", "sub-second" | Performance numbers age badly and sound like marketing. The product is fast; the video shows it rather than says it. |
| Broker names in VO-3 | "Six brokers" is true today and stays true if a seventh is added. Naming them dates the video. |
| The Machine ID, licensing mechanics | Support detail. Not why anyone buys. |
| OpenAlgo, Pine Script, macOS | Standing rules: not shipped / not sold / not on. |
| "AI" | Not what the product does, and the word is now a red flag to the audience you want. |
