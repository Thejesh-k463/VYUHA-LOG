# Tour narration — the 10-minute walkthrough

Owner-only · written 2026-08-28 against v2.99.104 · pairs with the takes that
`scripts/demo-video/record.mjs` films automatically (one `.webm` per block,
same numbering). Same rules as the short video's narration: every claim
verified against the code, **no outcome claims** (SEBI posture — this file is
scanned by `tests/demo-video-copy.test.ts`), numbers written as words where
the voice should say words.

**How to use:** Clipchamp → Text to speech → English (India) → paste ONE block
→ speed 0.9× → rename `T-n`. Thirteen clips (intro + eleven scenes + close).

---

## T-0 · Cold open
*~18 s · plays over the first seconds of take 01*

Most trading journals tell you what you made. Vyuha is built to tell you the
truth — what it actually cost, where the leaks are, and what your own record
says when it isn't flattering you. It runs entirely on your computer. No
account. No cloud. Nothing leaves your machine. This is the full tour.

---

## T-1 · Dashboard
*~35 s · take 01-dashboard*

This is the dashboard — your whole book at a glance. The equity curve, the
daily calendar, and the numbers that matter, each computed from your actual
fills, never typed in. Every card here is a door: click it, and it shows you
exactly how it was calculated and what sits inside it. Nothing in Vyuha is a
number you have to take on faith — that is the design rule the whole product
is built on.

---

## T-2 · Portfolio Risk
*~40 s · take 02-portfolio-risk*

Portfolio Risk is the live cockpit: what you are exposed to, right now. Open
positions with the rupee figure beside every percentage. Each position opens
into its own panel — entry, mark, allocation, days held — and this is where
you manage the trade: set a stop, a trailing stop, a target, and the cockpit
recomputes your open risk as you type. Trail to break-even is one click.

---

## T-3 · Accounts
*~40 s · take 03-accounts*

If you trade more than one account — your own, family, different brokers —
Vyuha keeps each one a separate book. Watch the switch: every number, every
report, every position changes with it. Nothing bleeds between books. There is
also an all-accounts view for the full picture — and it is read-only by
design, so an import can never land in the wrong book. Adding an account is a
name and a click, in Settings.

---

## T-4 · Calculator and Session Plan
*~30 s · take 04-calculator-session*

Before the trade: the calculator sizes a position from your risk, not from a
feeling — and it remembers how you work. And Session Plan is the page you fill
before the bell: what you will trade, what you will not, and the line you will
not cross today. It is there in the evening, holding you to it.

---

## T-5 · Trades
*~55 s · take 05-trades*

The journal itself. Every trade with its charges, its R multiple, its tags —
and underneath each one, the fills you actually made. Scale into a position
over a week, and Vyuha keeps the ladder — every tranche at its real price,
never blended away into one average. Attach the chart you traded from, so the
record holds the evidence, not just the numbers. And when something never made
it into a broker file — record it by hand in seconds, closed or still open.
The journal is free, forever. Recording your own trades is never something
Vyuha charges for.

---

## T-6 · Arjun's Eye
*~30 s · take 06-arjuns-eye*

Arjun's Eye asks the uncomfortable questions. When are you actually good — and
when are you just busy? Which hour of the day earns, and which one quietly
bleeds? It reads your record and shows you the pattern you cannot see from
inside it.

---

## T-7 · Lenses, and deleting with intent
*~30 s · take 07-lenses-delete*

Lenses group your book any way you think — by setup, by sector, by theme, by
mistake — and show what each slice is really doing. And when you need to
remove trades, you delete by scope, deliberately: a preview of exactly what
goes, a snapshot taken first, and nothing silently lost.

---

## T-8 · Import
*~55 s · take 08-import*

Getting your trades in is the part everyone dreads — so this is where Vyuha
works hardest. Drop the file your broker gave you. Six brokers are recognised
automatically, by what is inside the file — any other broker goes through a
column mapper that asks once and remembers. Before anything is saved, Vyuha
recomputes every statutory charge from your own rate card and shows its number
beside the broker's. Then commit — and re-imports never double a trade: if
something is already in your journal, Vyuha says so and shows you exactly
which trades matched. Four brokers can also connect live and pull the day's
fills themselves.

---

## T-9 · Charges and Broker Costs
*~35 s · take 09-charges-broker-costs*

This is the report your broker will never send you: every rupee of charges,
by type, over time — and what margin funding is really costing. Broker Costs
puts brokers side by side on your actual trading, not on a pricing page. The
computed figures here have been checked against brokers' own signed contract
notes — down to the exact paisa on the securities transaction tax.

---

## T-10 · Tax
*~35 s · take 10-tax*

Come March, the tax pack is already built. The summary your C A can read, the
I T R schedules, advance-tax dates, harvesting candidates. And one honest
detail worth noticing: where Vyuha cannot derive a number, it leaves the cell
blank. It will not write a zero it cannot defend.

---

## T-11 · Settings and Backup
*~30 s · take 11-settings-backup*

Make it yours — nine skins, light or dark, tuned so the numbers stay readable
in every one of them. And your entire journal is one file on your machine:
back it up with one click, move it, restore it. If a restore ever fails, your
journal is left exactly as it was. Your data behaves like your property,
because it is.

---

*~20 s · plays over the end card*

Every fresh install starts with a seven-day full trial. No sign-up. No card.
The core journal stays free forever — the licence unlocks the intelligence on
top. The link is below. Keep an honest book.
