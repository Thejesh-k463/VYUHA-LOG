The problem

Most Indian traders have no honest record of their own trading.

The broker shows P&L, not behaviour

Console and order books answer "how much". Nothing answers why: which setups, which hours, which mistakes, which instruments, and what the charges actually cost.

Journals built for the US do not fit India

Global journals are web-hosted subscriptions priced in dollars, with no STT/CTT, stamp duty, GST, exchange and SEBI charges, no Indian tax pack, and no MTF or options-seller workflow.

Web journals require trust they have not earned

Every existing product asks traders to upload their full trade history to someone else's server. Many traders — rightly — refuse.

Result: the trader who most needs to learn from their record is the one least likely to keep one. Vyuha exists to make the record effortless, exact, and theirs.

The solution

A desktop journal that computes the truth and keeps it on the trader's machine.

Local-first by design

  • Windows desktop app. SQLite file in the user's app-data. No account, no cloud, no telemetry — no Vyuha server exists.
  • Works fully offline. Backup and restore are one file.
  • The core journal — recording, every importer, backups — is free and never gated.

What ships today (v2.99.100)

  • Import: 6 auto-detected broker formats (Zerodha, Dhan, Groww, Angel One, Upstox, Paytm Money) + any CSV via a column mapper + 3 broker-API pulls (Kite Connect, Dhan, Angel One SmartAPI).
  • Charges engine: STT/CTT, stamp duty, GST, exchange & SEBI charges from a broker × segment × exchange rate table — within 0.69% of a real broker report across 92 verified rows.
  • Analytics: expectancy, R-multiples, setups, mistakes, time-of-day, sector and theme lenses, options-seller journal, risk, staged positions, MTF.
  • Tax pack: FY-wise P&L, STCG/LTCG, speculative vs non-speculative, ITR-schedule export.

Competitive advantages

Six claims — each is architecture or arithmetic, verifiable by reading the product. None is a promise about results.

Three-year cost is a subtraction, not a subscription

Lifetime is ₹29,999 once. Three years of the global journals runs ≈ ₹47,000–95,000 at their current prices.

Your data lives on your machine

There is no Vyuha server to send it to. Every competitor is a web app with a login; Vyuha is a desktop app with a file.

The only lifetime licence in the table

Every competitor listed is subscription-only. Vyuha is the one product you can stop paying for and keep.

Indian statutory charges are computed, not copied

STT/CTT, stamp duty, GST, exchange and SEBI charges from a broker × segment × exchange rate table — within 0.69% of a real broker report across 92 verified rows. Parsing charges a broker already reported is not computing them.

The core journal is never metered

Recording trades, every importer, backups — free forever, no monthly trade cap, no account cap. Free tiers elsewhere meter trades, executions or accounts.

Works with no internet at all

Journal on a train, in a dealing room, anywhere — the app never needs a connection to record or analyse.
