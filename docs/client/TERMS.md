# Terms of use

**Last updated:** 2026-08-29 · **Applies to:** Vyuha v3.0.0 and later

## 1. What you are buying

A licence to use Vyuha on your own computer. Two plans:

- **Pro — Annual.** Unlocks the Pro analytics for one year from the date your key
  is issued. It does not auto-renew.
- **Journal — Lifetime.** Unlocks the Pro analytics permanently for the versions
  released during the life of the product.

**One licence, one trader.** A key is issued to your email address and displays
it inside the app. You may install Vyuha on your own machines; you may not
share, resell or publish the key. Keys found posted publicly are withdrawn.

## 2. What is free, permanently

The core journal is **never** gated and never expires: recording trades,
importing broker files, the dashboard, staged positions, playbooks, backups and
exports. This holds whether you never buy, your annual licence lapses, or your
licence is withdrawn. Your record of your own trading is yours.

## 3. Not financial advice

Vyuha is a **record-keeping and analytics tool**. It does not provide investment,
trading or tax advice, does not recommend securities, and does not execute
orders. Every number it shows is derived from data you supplied.

**Tax figures are informational.** The tax pack, ITR schedules, advance-tax
estimates and turnover calculations are computed to the best of our
understanding of Indian tax rules and are labelled inside the app where they are
approximations. **Verify with a qualified professional before filing.** You are
responsible for your own returns.

## 4. Accuracy, and what depends on you

Charges are recomputed from **the rate card you configure**. If your broker's
rates are entered wrongly, every figure downstream will be wrong in the same
way. Market-data features (mark-to-market, Greeks, surveillance lists) depend on
files you supply; Greeks in particular are an approximation for decision
support, not a pricing-desk model, and the app says so where they appear.

## 5. Your data, and backups

Vyuha stores everything in a single database file on your computer. Nothing
about you or your trades is uploaded — see the privacy statement shipped
alongside this file.

**Backups are your responsibility.** Vyuha provides the export and restore tools
and takes an automatic backup before a database upgrade, but it cannot recover
data from a machine you no longer have. Take backups and keep them somewhere
else.

you. We do not promise a release schedule or that any particular feature will
