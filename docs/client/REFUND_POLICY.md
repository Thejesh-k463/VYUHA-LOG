# Refund policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-28 · **Applies to:** Vyuha v2.99.103 and later

## Try it first — that is the point of the trial

Every fresh install of Vyuha starts a **7-day full-Pro trial**. No signup, no
card, no account. It is the complete product for those seven days, not a
cut-down demo, and the clock starts the first time *you* open the app — not when
the installer was built.

We would rather you found out Vyuha is wrong for you during the trial than after
paying for it. Import your real broker file, run your real numbers, open the tax
pack. If it does not do what you need, walk away having spent nothing.

## After you buy

Because the trial gives you the entire product before payment, **purchases are
final once your licence key has been issued.**

Two exceptions, and we will honour both without argument:

1. **It does not work on your machine.** If Vyuha will not install, will not
   activate, or is broken in a way we cannot fix within **7 days** of your
   purchase, you get a full refund.
2. **You were charged in error** — a duplicate payment, or the wrong amount.
   Refunded in full, whenever you spot it.

To ask for either, message the WhatsApp number on your invoice with your **Key
ID** (Settings → License — the short `A1B2-C3D4-E5` code, never the full key)
and what went wrong.

## What a refund does

Your licence key is revoked, and Pro screens stop working after a dated warning
period shown inside the app. **Your journal is not affected**: every trade you
recorded stays readable, editable and exportable, exactly as it would on a free
copy. We do not hold your own data hostage — not at purchase, not at refund.

## What is not refundable

- A change of mind after the trial has ended and the licence has been used.
- An annual licence part-way through its year, because you decided to stop
  trading. The trial exists to prevent this.
- Anything to do with your trading results. Vyuha is a record-keeping and
  analytics tool; it does not give investment advice and makes no claim about
  your profitability.

## Renewals (Pro — Annual)

An annual licence stops at its expiry date. **It does not auto-renew, and there
is nothing to cancel** — no card is stored, because no card was ever taken. If
you do nothing, it simply lapses and the core journal keeps working forever.

---

<small>\*Beyond the two exceptions above, refunds are granted at the owner's
discretion. If tampering with the software, changes that replicate Vyuha, or any
other malpractice is verified, no refund will be provided and the licence may be
revoked.\*</small>
