How professionals actually build

Scale in. Scale out. Keep the maths honest.

1 · First entry
Your stop sets 1R —
and it stays fixed from here
→
2 · Add as it proves itself
Each tranche gets
its own stop
→
3 · Book part of it
25% · 50% · all —
the rest keeps running
→
4 · See per-leg R
Half at +2R, rest at +3R
= +2.5R
This is the screen professional position-builders live in: exits price against your blended average (so it matches your broker’s P&L), quantity retires oldest-first (so the surviving tranche keeps its stop), and R is frozen at your first entry — adding size never flatters the risk multiple.
