Privacy

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · VyuhaLens, Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh, India

This website

vyuhalens.com is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and has no forms. We do not collect anything about you when you read it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address to deliver and protect the service; that is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement, and VyuhaLens does not receive it.

When you contact us

If you email founder@vyuhalens.com (hosted on Zoho Mail) or message us on WhatsApp, we receive what you send — your name, address or number, and your message. We use it only to reply and, if you buy a licence, to issue and support it. We do not sell or share it, and we do not add you to marketing lists. To have your messages deleted, ask at the same address.

Our products

  • Vyuha keeps your journal on your own computer; VyuhaLens cannot see your trades. Its full privacy statement, including the parts that are not absolute, is here.
  • MARKET SENTINEL and GAMMA-UNWIND run on the buyer's own machine. What each one stores and sends is stated in the documents delivered with it.
