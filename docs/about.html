About VyuhaLens

VyuhaLens is a bootstrapped software company founded in July 2026 in Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh, India, by founder-developer K. Thejeswar Reddy.

What we do

Most Indian retail traders cannot see what their own trading actually costs or shows: charges are spread across contract notes, tax needs hand-built spreadsheets, and the patterns in a trader's record stay invisible. VyuhaLens builds tools that read the trader's own data and the exchanges' public data, and state what is there in plain language — every figure tied to where it came from.

Three products ship today:

  • Vyuha — a trade journal for Windows desktop: broker imports, statutory charges to the rupee, an ITR pack, and plain-language observations about the trader's own journal. Paid licences since August 2026.
  • MARKET SENTINEL — company results, management guidance, insider and bulk-deal activity and surveillance changes, delivered to the buyer's own Telegram.
  • GAMMA-UNWIND — F&O open-interest automation with a dashboard on the client's own computer.

They are being brought together into one desk for the Indian retail trader.

How we use AI

MARKET SENTINEL uses Anthropic's Claude to read NSE concall transcripts and filing PDFs and extract management guidance as verbatim quotes. Every quote must pass a verification check against the source document before a user sees it. Planned next: plain-language narration of a trader's own Vyuha journal, where every sentence is tied to the fact it came from. AI use is disclosed to users, AI output is presented as observation with its source shown, and no AI feature answers "what should I buy".
